# Origin of these PNGs

Copied unchanged from
<https://github.com/googlemaps-samples/android-navigation-samples> at commit
`a73bfbae075de72c8058d0b4df490612cb29078f` (2026-10-05), folder
`navigation-sample/app/src/main/res/drawable/`. The repository belongs to Google and is under the
**Apache License 2.0**; the license copy is the `LICENSE` next to them. Licensed, then.

`tools/glifos.py --download` (alias `--baixar`) fetches them again at the same commit. What goes to the watch is
derived from them (traced into polygons and recolored): `watch/source/render/TurnGlyphs.mc` and
`tools/glifos/glifos.json`.
